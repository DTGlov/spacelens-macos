# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SpaceLens is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience level, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Give and receive technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's files, paths, logs, or personal information.
- Prioritize user safety when discussing filesystem and deletion behavior.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Insults, threats, trolling, or targeted personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging unsafe or deceptive behavior toward users or contributors.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the SpaceLens project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using a private contact method listed on the [DTGlov GitHub profile](https://github.com/DTGlov). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.
